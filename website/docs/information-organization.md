# Information Organization Skills for Emotional and Social Moments

Emotional and social literacy uses signals, patterns, scripts, boundaries, trust, repair, screenshots, rumors, reflections, and group dynamics. Information Organization can help kids notice patterns and prepare tools — while still protecting privacy and safety.

This page is the local doorway into the Literacy for Kids [Information Organization Toolkit](https://www.literacy-for-kids.com/docs/toolkits/information-organization), connected to the emotional and social skills this curriculum builds.

## A few core ideas

- **Signals and stories are different kinds of information.** Sorting them helps you see what actually happened.
- **Patterns are easier to notice over time.** A simple note beats a perfect memory.
- **Scripts can be saved for future use.** A labeled script is easier to find in a hard moment.
- **Private information deserves privacy.** Nobody is required to share personal notes.
- **Screenshots, rumors, and messages need trusted adult support when unsafe or serious.**
- **Kids should not be required to share personal social details to learn the skill.** Fictional examples work fine.

## When this shows up

- When noticing body clues or feelings
- When tracking a fictional pattern
- When preparing a boundary script
- When sorting signal vs. story
- When thinking about trust patterns
- When a rumor, screenshot, or group conflict feels too big

## Tools that help

- **Signal / story sort** — what my body reported vs. what my brain added.
- **Pattern note** — small, private, over time.
- **Boundary script label** — named so future-you can find it.
- **Trust pattern organizer** — what someone has shown more than once.
- **Private information check** — does this stay private, and who can help?
- **Trusted adult support** — for anything serious or unsafe.

:::tip Information Organization Moment
Private information still needs care. If a message, screenshot, rumor, or conflict feels serious or unsafe, do not try to organize it alone. Save what a trusted adult needs to help, and ask for support.
:::

:::caution Everyday organization skills, not unsafe conflict management
These are everyday information organization tools, not therapy, bullying intervention, conflict mediation, or a replacement for trusted adults, educators, caregivers, or qualified support. Kids should not be expected to organize, share, or solve unsafe social situations alone. Private messages, screenshots, health information, and personal conflicts should be handled with trusted adult support.
:::
